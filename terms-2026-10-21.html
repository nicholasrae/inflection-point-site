You retain ownership of your content and are responsible for it. The app stores the journey locally and does not provide an app-managed cloud-sync account. Apple device backups are separate platform services. The guided reset saves morning responses, daytime pattern checks and evening reviews in that local journey. You review and explicitly create a plan before the app adds its linked goals, projects, quests and Synthesis summary. Optional reminder times are managed separately in Settings.

Earlier Expo versions provide partial JSON export of identity, quests and daily logs. The earlier native beta already includes journey export during full access. These journal records are also included in local recovery backups. Export and recovery of existing data do not require a purchase. Purchase, historical preview/usage and reminder preferences are excluded from the journey export. Keep private copies safe. Native import replaces this device’s journey after confirmation.

Unsaved drafts are not saved journal entries, and this does not guarantee survival after force-quitting, a crash or device restart. Treat an edit as saved only after the app confirms a successful save.

Build 11 Reset all data removes the local journey, including guided-reset responses, reviews and applied-plan records, and app-owned recovery copies, backups, avatars and local exports, while retaining purchase and historical preview/usage records. It does not remove exported copies elsewhere, Apple backups or provider-held records. Available edit and deletion controls differ by version.

5. Optional AI output

In build 11, optional daily AI debriefs use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. They require an Apple Intelligence-eligible iPhone or iPad, supported language and region, Apple Intelligence enabled and the model ready. The app does not upload debrief input for generation or use a cloud debrief fallback. Manual Quick Log remains available when AI is unavailable or a request fails.

Optional avatars use Apple’s system Image Playground after agreement to share selected text and identity context. The app offers Apple’s Illustration, Animation and Sketch styles without an external-provider style. On iOS 27 and iPadOS 27, Apple’s image models use Private Cloud Compute, separate from the on-device debrief; no offline avatar-generation promise is made. Photo and camera choices remain alternatives. Earlier Expo versions and native betas used Rork for avatars and online debriefs; see the Privacy Policy for those version differences.

AI output can be incomplete, inaccurate or unsuitable for important decisions. Review the output and ensure you have the right to submit the content you use. Provider-specific retention, training and deletion terms have not been confirmed for historical Rork avatar requests and online debriefs; the Privacy Policy explains that limitation. Apple’s system generation has its own privacy information and availability conditions. Online generation depends on provider terms, capacity and availability. An alignment score is reflective feedback, not a clinical assessment or an objective measure of your worth.
