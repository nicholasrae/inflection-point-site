## Provider and release gates

Official toolkit documentation identifies structured and image-generation providers, but it does not confirm the terms for the app's raw endpoint requests. The general builder policy and upstream OpenAI defaults do not settle Rork-layer retention, training, human review, deletion, advertising use, routing or subprocessors. Corrected public copy states those limits without a fixed retention/deletion deadline or no-training/no-human-access promise.

An endpoint-specific request draft is saved externally and **has not been sent**. Applicable contracts, actual provider configuration and category-specific Health & Body / Wealth & Career disclosures remain prerequisites to public AI release. No provider, customer or other recipient was contacted. Older policy draft PR #1 remains separate; these website corrections do not announce the planned StoreKit app as shipped or replace app privacy/release review.

The website can be available with truthful planned-release information while app distribution remains gated. Archive/signature success, an internal beta, an email-request CTA and source-level migration behavior establish different things; none proves public app availability or completed physical purchase/upgrade QA.
