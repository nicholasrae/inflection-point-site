Inflection Point

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 16, 2026 · Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Inflection Point ("we", "us", or "our") is an identity-based planning and reflection app. This policy describes the native Swift app's handling of your information, including its optional online features.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the content you enter: your character name, visions and anti-visions, reasons and traits, goals, projects, quests, constraints, boss challenges, morning exercises, daily reflections, wins, intentions, and progress. It also stores reminder preferences, purchase access, preview dates, and daily usage limits.

You can choose a profile image using the system photo picker or camera. The app copies that image into its own local storage. It does not upload your chosen photo or camera image for AI generation, purchase processing, or analytics. Camera access is requested when you choose to take a photo.

There is no Inflection Point account login, app-managed cloud sync, or in-app iCloud backup service. Apple device backups may include app data depending on your settings. Those backups are separate from the app's local recovery backup and are governed by Apple's backup information.

Optional AI debriefs and avatars

Manual reflection and planning do not send journal content to an AI provider. Online generation occurs only when you choose the relevant feature, agree to its data-sharing explanation, and initiate a request.

For an AI debrief, the app sends your reflection text, selected wins, selected violated constraints, character name, active traits and anti-traits, visions and their reasons, and anti-visions and fear levels to Rork AI toolkit services. It sends the context needed for that request, rather than your entire stored reflection history.

For an AI avatar, the app sends the character name, selected traits, and vision descriptions you choose to include, together with an image-generation prompt. You can omit name and vision context. Your existing profile photo is not included. Generated reports and images are returned to the app; a report becomes part of your local journal when you save it.

These requests use HTTPS. An online service receives ordinary network information such as your IP address when servicing a request. Rork and any providers it uses may process submitted context and generated output. See Rork's Privacy Policy.

We do not promise that AI providers discard prompts immediately, never retain them, or never use them for training. Toolkit-specific retention, downstream provider, and training terms have not been confirmed. Avoid submitting information you do not want processed by an online service. You can continue using manual reflection without using these features.

Purchases and platform services

Apple processes payments. The app does not receive your full payment-card information. RevenueCat validates purchases, supplies lifetime access and restore information, and provides purchase analytics. RevenueCat uses an automatically generated app user identifier and purchase records; its service may also receive technical information such as device type, operating system, and app version. The app does not send your journal, profile image, name, or email as RevenueCat customer attributes. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

The Swift app does not integrate advertising, advertising identifiers, or a third-party crash or session analytics SDK. Apple may handle platform diagnostics under your Apple privacy settings. Opening a legal or support link takes you to an external browser or email service, which has its own practices. If you email support, we receive the address, message, and attachments you choose to send.

Reminders

Reminder preferences and scheduling are local. The app uses Apple's local notifications after permission is granted; it does not register a push token or send your journal to a remote notification service. Notification messages use general prompts rather than the text of your journal. You can change reminders in Settings or turn off notification permission in iOS Settings.

Storage, retention, and security

Journal files and avatars are stored in the app's device sandbox. Native saves use iOS file protection and retain a previous valid local backup. This is not a separate end-to-end encrypted cloud journal. Device security and your passcode settings affect protection. No system can guarantee complete security.

An upgrade can copy your legacy app journal and avatar into native storage. The original legacy journal is retained for recovery until you choose Reset all data. A damaged journal is preserved while you choose recovery rather than being replaced with blank data. Older entries remain on the device when the free tier limits visible history.

Local records remain until you reset the app or remove its local data. Removing an avatar from your identity stops displaying it; older app-owned image files may remain until Reset all data. Reset all data removes the app-owned journey, recovery files, journal backups, avatars, and local export files. It preserves purchase ownership and preview/usage records so it does not revoke a purchase or restart the preview. Copies you previously exported, Apple device backups, and records retained by service providers are not deleted by this action.

Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records under their own service and legal requirements. AI-provider retention is not controlled by the app, and no fixed retention period is promised here. Online processing may occur in countries other than your own under the applicable providers' practices.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can edit your identity and today's reflection, remove the displayed avatar, control reminders, choose manual reflection, and reset the local journey. Complete JSON export is available during the full-access preview or with lifetime access; it contains your journal and an embedded avatar if present. Import replaces this device's journey only after confirmation. Treat export and recovery files as private and share them only with recipients you choose.

For access, correction, deletion, or other rights available under applicable law, contact us using the address below. We can help with records under our control and appropriate provider requests. We cannot remotely retrieve a journal that exists only on your device. Do not send journal content or a recovery file to support unless you choose to share it.

The app is not designed as a children's service. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an online feature, contact us so we can review the request with the relevant provider.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when practices change and provide additional notice where required. The current published policy is at inflectionpoint.me/privacy.html.
