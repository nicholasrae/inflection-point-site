04 / A few answers

Before you begin.

Can I download it now?+

The App Store release is being prepared. Request a launch update to hear when it becomes available. A public release date has not been announced.

Is the preview a subscription?+

No. The 72-hour preview is timed access inside the app. It does not automatically charge you. After it ends, you can continue with the free limits or choose a one-time lifetime purchase.

What happens to older reflections?+

In the planned release, free access shows the most recent seven days. Older entries remain saved on your device; lifetime access lets you view the full history.

Do I have to use AI?+

You can write synthesis entries manually. AI debriefs and AI avatar generation are optional online features that send relevant text and identity context to AI services when you request them. Read the Privacy Policy before sharing information.

How do I restore a lifetime purchase?+

Use Restore Purchases in Settings with the Apple account that owns the eligible lifetime purchase. Purchase and restore require Apple’s services.

How do launch updates work?+

The request button opens an email to the developer. Send that email to ask for an update when the app is available. You can also use it to ask a question.
