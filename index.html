01 / A guided reset

From reflection
to a plan.

Make space to notice what needs to change. Move through the day at your own pace, then choose what you want to do next.

  1. 01

    Morning: look at the pattern.

    Eight prompts help you reflect on your habits, identity and direction. Save your progress, pause and return when you’re ready.

  2. 02

    Daytime: notice it in real life.

    Record short pattern check-ins as the day unfolds. Optional reminders help you make room for the practice on your schedule.

  3. 03

    Evening: choose tomorrow’s direction.

    Bring your reflections into a yearly goal, a monthly project and two or three actions for tomorrow. Review everything before choosing Create my plan. You write it; you decide what to save.

A manual exercise, with no AI required. Inspired by Dan Koe’s one-day reset article. Inflection Point is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by the author.

05 / A few answers

Before you begin.

Can I download it now?+

Yes, you can join the iPhone and iPad beta on TestFlight. Open the link on your iPhone or iPad, install Apple’s TestFlight app if needed, and follow the invitation to join. The public App Store release is still in preparation; a release date has not been announced.

Which devices are supported?+

The current beta supports iPhone with iOS 26 or later and iPad with iPadOS 26 or later. Manual planning, the guided reset and Quick Log work without Apple Intelligence. Optional AI features have additional hardware, language, region and availability requirements, described below.

Is the iPhone Duo update available?+

An iPhone Duo update is in preparation. The Duo simulator previews show build 12, which is awaiting external TestFlight review as of October 22, 2026. The later Quests and Synthesis layout and draft-continuity improvements have not yet been uploaded. The build offered by TestFlight may differ while Apple reviews new versions.

How does payment work?+

The planned release uses one lifetime purchase to unlock the protocol. There is no free trial, ongoing free tier or recurring subscription. The planned US price is US$14.99; Apple’s localized price is shown before you purchase.

During the beta, purchases in TestFlight use Apple’s sandbox and are free of charge. They don’t carry over as production App Store purchases. Existing eligible lifetime ownership and preserved historical paid access are separate from beta test transactions. See Apple’s TestFlight purchase guidance.

What happens to older reflections?+

The planned lifetime purchase includes your full saved reflection history. Older saved entries remain on your device. Earlier Expo versions could delete older entries; upgrading cannot recover entries already removed. See the Privacy Policy for version differences.

What’s in a journey export?+

The planned release provides complete JSON export of your journal collections, including guided reset responses, daytime check-ins and reviewed plans, with an embedded avatar when present. Export and recovery of existing data remain available without a purchase. Purchase, historical preview/usage and reminder preferences are separate. Earlier Expo exports were partial; see the Privacy Policy for version differences.

Do I have to use AI?+

The guided reset and Quick Log are written by you; no AI is required. The planned native release uses Apple Intelligence for an optional daily debrief on your device, with no cloud debrief fallback. It requires an eligible iPhone or iPad, supported language and region, Apple Intelligence enabled, and the model ready. Use Quick Log when it is unavailable.

Optional avatars use Apple’s system Image Playground after you agree to share selected text and identity context. This is separate from the on-device debrief: on iOS 27 and iPadOS 27, Apple’s image generation uses Private Cloud Compute and system usage limits. You can use a photo or camera instead. Read the Privacy Policy before sharing information.

How do I restore a lifetime purchase?+

Choose Restore purchases on the Lifetime Access screen, or in Settings if you already have access. Use the Apple account that owns the eligible lifetime purchase. Purchase and restore require Apple’s services.

How do I send beta feedback?+

Open Inflection Point in the TestFlight app and choose Send Beta Feedback. Share what felt useful, confusing or broken; include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version and steps to reproduce an issue. You can also contact the developer below.

Settings also lets you preview and share a local support report. Review it before sending; sharing is your choice. Read the Privacy Policy for what the report includes and the diagnostic controls available.
