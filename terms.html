Manual Quick Log remains available when AI is unavailable or a request fails.

The app offers Apple’s Illustration, Animation and Sketch styles without an external-provider style. On iOS 27, Apple’s image models use Private Cloud Compute, separate from the on-device debrief; no offline avatar-generation promise is made. Photo and camera choices remain alternatives. Earlier Expo versions and native betas used Rork for avatars and online debriefs; see the Privacy Policy for those version differences.

AI output can be incomplete, inaccurate or unsuitable for important decisions. Review the output and ensure you have the right to submit the content you use.
